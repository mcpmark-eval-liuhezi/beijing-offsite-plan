# Beijing Half-Day Team Offsite — Itinerary

**Team size:** 6 · **Format:** morning work session (Zhongguancun) → lunch → optional Jingshan Park hill climb
**Starting point:** hotel near Beijing Railway Station, Dongcheng District

---

## 1. Morning Work Session — Cafe Shortlist (Zhongguancun, Haidian District)

All three are well-rated cafes in the Zhongguancun area with enough seating for six people to sit and work. Details verified via Google Maps.

### Recommended: Sculpting in Time Café (雕刻时光咖啡馆)

- **Address:** 3F, Ou Mei Hui Shopping Center, Jia-1 Danling Street (丹棱街甲1号), Zhongguancun, Haidian District, Beijing 100086, China
- **Rating:** 4.5 / 5
- **Opening hours:** Daily, 10:00 – 22:00
- **Phone:** +86 10 8248 4551
- **Why:** the highest-rated cafe in the area, dine-in with full lunch/dinner service, in a relaxed mall setting; opens at 10:00, so plan the session for **10:00 – 13:00**.

### Backup 1: Starbucks Coffee (Zhongguancun Plaza)

- **Address:** B1, Zone A, Zhongguancun Plaza Shopping Mall, 15 Zhongguancun Street (中关村大街15号), Haidian District, Beijing 100080, China
- **Rating:** 3.9 / 5
- **Opening hours:** Daily, 10:00 – 22:00
- **Why:** reliable, spacious and predictable; dependable Wi-Fi and seating for six.

### Backup 2: Garage Café (车库咖啡)

- **Address:** Zhongguancun, Haidian District, Beijing 100086 (listing carries no street address — call to confirm exact entrance)
- **Rating:** 3.8 / 5
- **Opening hours:** Daily, 9:00 – 22:00
- **Why:** opens earliest (9:00) and is a well-known startup co-working cafe — tables and power are the whole point of the place. Use this one if we want a 9:00 start.

## 4. Afternoon (Optional) — Jingshan Park Hill Climb

- **Park address:** Jingshan Park, 44 Jingshan West Street (景山西街44号), Xicheng District, Beijing 100009
- **From lunch:** 13.4 km, about **35 minutes by car** (~45–55 min by metro, Line 4→Line 6/National Art Museum area — same transit caveat applies)
- **How high is the hill?** Jingshan (Coal Hill) rises roughly **45 metres above the surrounding city**, reaching about **90 m above sea level** at the summit where the Wanchun Pavilion (万春亭) stands. It is a short, easy climb — about **10–15 minutes on foot** — and the payoff is the classic panoramic view over the Forbidden City and central Beijing. A nice, low-effort team finale.
