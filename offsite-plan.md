## ✅ Recommendation: 北京中国大饭店 — China World Hotel (Shangri-La)

**Address:** 中国北京市朝阳区建国门外大街1号 (1 Jianguomenwai Ave, Chaoyang District, 100004) · +86 10 6505 2266
**Rating:** 4.4 (401 reviews)

**Why it wins:**

1. **One roof, two needs.** The half-day session and the group dinner both need to work, and a full-service five-star hotel is the only category on this list that reliably does both: dedicated conference/meeting space of the size a team offsite needs, plus several in-house restaurants — including the hotel's Chinese restaurant with private dining rooms — so there is no mid-evening venue shuffle for a jet-lagged group.
2. **Highest review volume at the second-highest rating.** 4.4 across 401 reviews is a far more statistically solid signal than a 4.5 across 40; on the day, the risk of a bad surprise is lowest here.
3. **Guomao is the easiest part of Beijing for people flying in.** It is a direct Line 1 / Line 10 interchange (Guomao station) and sits right on the Airport Express corridor, so it is the least painful arrival and dispersal point in this shortlist.

**Runner-up:** 长安壹号 is the strongest pure-dinner option and is by far the closest to the anchor (1.2 km, 26 minutes on foot — genuinely walkable), but it depends on sourcing separate meeting space, which splits the logistics.

### Walking route from the anchor to the top pick

5.4 km, about **1 hour 24 minutes** on foot — a pleasant one-off stroll through the old Legation Quarter rather than a commute. Route: south from the anchor along 正义路 onto 东交民巷, left onto 北京站西街, left onto 北京站街, then east along 建内大街 continuing onto 建国门外大街, arriving at No. 1 Jianguomenwai Ave. (For day-to-day movement, take the metro: Line 1 eastbound from Tiananmen East to Guomao.)
